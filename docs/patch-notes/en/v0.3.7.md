# JYSV HERA 0.3.7 — 2026-11-10

Usage numbers now keep up, count each response once, and include Antigravity.

### Added

- **Antigravity token usage.** HERA now reads the conversation logs Antigravity keeps on your computer and shows its tokens by day and by folder, like Claude Code and Codex. The logs do not name the model, so usage appears under one `Antigravity` row, and they hold no remaining limit or cost, so those stay empty. Antigravity keeps only its newest 500 conversations. Once HERA has counted one, it stays counted after Antigravity deletes it, but conversations deleted before this update cannot be recovered.

### Fixed

- **Usage could stop updating when you have a lot of logs.** On a machine with 42 GB of logs, a full count took 162 seconds against a 180-second limit and used 13.7 GB of memory. It now takes about 43 seconds and 200 MB, the limit is 5 minutes, and a count that gets cut off carries on from where it stopped.
- **A failed count no longer costs you the numbers.** HERA keeps showing the last ones and tries again at the next refresh. If they are more than 10 minutes old, the Usage page says so.
- **Limits in the status bar were up to 5 minutes old.** They now refresh every minute, and a limit past its reset time no longer shows its old percentage.
- **Claude Code numbers were off in two ways.** A subagent that starts from a copy of its parent's conversation had that copy counted again, and with Claude Code 2.1.289 and 2.1.296 a subagent's output tokens could be nearly lost. Each response now counts once across all logs, at its final output.
- **Some Codex sessions were left out** when they only wrote the newer kind of token record.
- **Work could be filed under no folder** when a log had not named its folder yet. HERA now keeps looking for it as the log grows.

After you update, the first count reads your logs again from the start. With very large logs that takes about a minute, and the Usage page fills in when it finishes.
